Chemical Form
Chemical Code must be at least 2 characters. Chemical Code cannot exceed 50 characters. Chemical Code cannot contain only spaces.

Active Ingredient is required. Active Ingredient must be at least 2 characters. Active Ingredient cannot exceed 255 characters. Active Ingredient cannot contain only spaces.
Unit is required. Unit must be at least 2 characters. Unit cannot exceed 50 characters. Unit cannot contain only spaces.
Stock Quantity is required. Stock Quantity must be at least 2 characters. Stock Quantity cannot exceed 100 characters. Stock Quantity cannot contain only spaces.
Reorder Level is required. Reorder Level must be at least 2 characters. Reorder Level cannot exceed 100 characters. Reorder Level cannot contain only spaces.
Expiry Date is required.
Safety Notes is required. Safety Notes must be at least 2 characters. Safety Notes cannot exceed 500 characters. Safety Notes cannot contain only spaces.
Chemical Status is required.
